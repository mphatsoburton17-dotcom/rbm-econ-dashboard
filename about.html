What this is

This is a working prototype that turns the Reserve Bank of Malawi's own published economic releases — the monthly Consumer Price Indices infographics and MPC Dashboard — into a single, interactive tool. Instead of a new static image each month, this site updates and lets anyone explore inflation, policy rates, and growth trends over time, with a plain-language explanation next to each figure.

It is meant as a companion to RBM's existing communications materials, not a replacement — the same trusted numbers, presented in a form that's easier to explore, share, and understand.

Who built it

Built independently by a software engineer who also runs a digital marketing agency, as a demonstration project — not commissioned or requested by RBM. It's offered freely, with the hope of contributing to RBM's public communication work, ideally through an internship or volunteer arrangement.

Why this approach

Central banks elsewhere already do this. The Bank of England runs a public inflation calculator. The Bank of Ghana maintains an open statistical database, mandated by their central bank act. South Africa's Reserve Bank offers an online statistical query tool. Malawi's own data is already high quality and well-designed — this project simply extends its reach to people who wouldn't otherwise see it.

What's next

Planned additions include an SMS service, so anyone can text a short code and receive the current inflation figure — no smartphone or internet required. This would make the data reachable for people in rural areas, who are often the most affected by inflation but least likely to see a website.

Independent prototype built from the Reserve Bank of Malawi's own public releases. Not affiliated with or endorsed by RBM.
