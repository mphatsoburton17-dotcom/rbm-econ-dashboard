Treasury Bill Comparison Real, sourced values

Which tenor pays more — and is it worth it?

Latest published RBM Treasury Bill auction yields, compared against current inflation to show what you're actually earning after prices rise.
TenorNominal YieldReal Yield*Auction Date
*Real Yield = Nominal Yield − current headline inflation. This is what you actually earn in buying-power terms, not just the sticker rate.
How to actually buy one: Treasury Bills are bought through RBM at auction, usually via a commercial bank or licensed dealer acting on your behalf. The minimum bid is typically MWK 10,000, in multiples of MWK 1,000 above that. Ask your bank about their T-bill purchase process for the exact current minimum and any fees.

Farmer Price Trends Real, sourced values

What crops actually paid, and how that's trended

Average price per kg actually paid for major export crops, as published by RBM/NSO/Ministry of Agriculture — the real market picture, year over year.
What this tool doesn't do: it shows what crops actually paid, not what you personally would earn. Turning that into a "type in your farm size, get your expected profit" calculator would need cost-of-production data — fertilizer, labor, land, yield per hectare — that varies enormously farm to farm and isn't something we can source reliably. Rather than guess those numbers and present them as fact, this tool sticks to real published prices so you can make your own informed decision.
These tools use RBM's own published rates but are independent estimates, not financial advice. Loan cost figures are based on the current Policy Rate only — actual bank loans include their own added margin. Always confirm figures with your bank or a licensed advisor before making a financial decision.
