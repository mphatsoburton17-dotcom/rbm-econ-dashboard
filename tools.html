Import Price Pass-Through Estimate

How much of a price rise could be the exchange rate?

If you import goods or inputs, this shows how much of a price change since you last paid could plausibly be explained by MWK depreciation against the USD, using this site's exchange rate data. It is an illustration of the FX effect only — not a forecast, and not the same as your actual cost.
Enter what you paid and how long ago, then press Estimate.
This tool only isolates the currency effect. Actual import prices are also driven by supplier costs, customs duties, shipping, and local demand — this estimate does not account for those.
Reserves Cover Calculator Real, sourced values

How many months of imports could Malawi's reserves cover?

This shows how many months Malawi could keep paying for imports using only its foreign currency reserves, if no new foreign currency came in. Economists often treat 3 months as a rough safety threshold — below that is generally considered risky.
